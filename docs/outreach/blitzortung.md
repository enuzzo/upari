# Blitzortung.org: lightning around the whole world

**To:** Blitzortung.org (contact page at https://www.blitzortung.org)
**Subject:** Showing Blitzortung lightning on Upari, a free open-source globe

---

Dear Blitzortung team,

I make Upari (https://upari.earth), a 3D globe that tells the Earth as it is today. It is
free, with no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no paid tier, and its code is open source
under the Apache License 2.0 (https://github.com/enuzzo/upari). It shows only open data, and
every source is credited on screen, with a link to its holder, wherever its data appear.

Upari has a lightning chapter: twenty years of the LIS/OTD climatology and the last half
hour seen by the GOES Geostationary Lightning Mappers. GLM covers only the Americas and the
Atlantic, so Africa, Europe and Asia stay dark, and that is exactly where your network of
volunteers is strongest. I understand that your data are reserved to station operators and
to non-commercial use, so I am asking before doing anything.

Would you allow Upari to show the strokes of your network in this limited form?

- once a day, the strokes of a recent half hour (or a coarser grid, if you prefer), fetched
  by the public build;
- drawn as flashes on the globe, positions only, rounded to 0.1°;
- never offered as a download or an API, and credited "Lightning: Blitzortung.org and its
  volunteer station operators" on the page.

I would also gladly add a line inviting readers to host a station. If the answer is yes, a
one-line reply is enough, and I will record it, with the date, in the project's third-party
notices. If you prefer, I can operate a station myself.
