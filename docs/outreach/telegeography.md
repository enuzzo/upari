# TeleGeography: submarine cables

**To:** TeleGeography (contact page at https://www2.telegeography.com)
**Subject:** Submarine cable routes on Upari, a free open-source globe

---

Dear TeleGeography team,

I make Upari (https://upari.earth), a 3D globe that tells the Earth as it is today. It is
free, with no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no paid tier, and its code is open source
under the Apache License 2.0 (https://github.com/enuzzo/upari). It shows only open data, and
every source is credited on the page that shows it.

Upari shows the shipping lanes and the lights of the night. The cables on the sea floor that
carry almost all of the internet would close that story, and your Submarine Cable Map is the
reference everybody quotes. I know the geocoded data are a product you sell, so I am asking
whether there is a way for a free, non-profit project to show them.

Would you allow Upari to show the cable routes in this limited form?

- the routes as thin lines of light on the globe, drawn once from a snapshot and refreshed
  when you allow it, with no names, owners or capacities;
- never offered as a download or an API;
- credited "Submarine cables: TeleGeography, submarinecablemap.com" on the page, with a link
  to your map for anyone who wants the details.

If the answer is yes, a one-line reply is enough, and I will record it, with the date, in
the project's third-party notices.
