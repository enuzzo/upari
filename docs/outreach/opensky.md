# OpenSky Network: aircraft in the sky

**To:** OpenSky Network association (contact form and address at https://opensky-network.org)
**Subject:** One daily snapshot of OpenSky positions on Upari, a free open-source globe

---

Dear OpenSky team,

I make Upari (https://upari.earth), a 3D globe that tells the Earth as it is today. It is
free, with no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no paid tier, and its code is open source
under the Apache License 2.0 (https://github.com/enuzzo/upari). It shows only open data, and
every source is credited on screen, with a link to its holder, wherever its data appear.

Upari already shows the ships of the world and the satellites overhead. The sky of people,
the aircraft, is missing, and OpenSky is the only open source of it. Your licence is limited
to non-profit research and education, and Upari is neither a lab nor a school, although it
is used in classrooms (it has a mode for teachers) and it is non-profit in every practical
sense. It carries the name of my small design studio as a signature; the studio earns
nothing from it.

May Upari show OpenSky data in this limited form?

- one snapshot a day of the state vectors (positions only, no callsigns or registrations
  shown), fetched by the public build in a single anonymous request, or with an account if
  you prefer;
- drawn as dots of light on the globe, so the reader sees how dense the sky is over Europe,
  North America and East Asia, and how empty over the oceans;
- never offered as a download or an API; credited "Aircraft positions: The OpenSky Network,
  https://opensky-network.org" with the citation of Schäfer et al. 2014.

If the answer is yes, a one-line reply is enough, and I will record it, with the date, in
the project's third-party notices.
