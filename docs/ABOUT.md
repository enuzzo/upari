**Kicker:** Why Upari

## Free as rain.

Upari is the Earth as it is right now. Today's winds, yesterday's sea, this week's fires
and earthquakes, the Space Station sliding overhead while you read this line. Nothing is
painted in: what you see was measured by satellites and sensors and handed down to anyone
who cares to look. We just learned to listen.

We made it to do three things, in this order: amaze you, inform you, and make you fall
hopelessly in love with this ridiculous, spinning, one-of-a-kind rock. In that order,
because nobody ever defended something that bored them.

### What it costs

Nothing. It's free, the way sunlight is free, the way rain is free, the way the wind
crossed an ocean this morning just to mess up your hair and never sent an invoice. No ads,
no accounts, nobody following you around.

We ask for one thing in return, and it isn't cheap:

**Look at it with the eyes of a child.**
**Defend it with the courage of a warrior.**

Both at once, ideally. Children are fearless, and every warrior was a child once.

### Who we are

We're Netmilk Studio. We make websites for a living and, now and then, something like
this for no better reason than love. Upari is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence:
take it apart, learn from it, make it better. There's only one planet. The code, at least,
can be forked.
