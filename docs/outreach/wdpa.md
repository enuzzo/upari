# WDPA: the world's protected areas

**To:** Protected Planet team, UNEP-WCMC, protectedareas@unep-wcmc.org
**Subject:** Showing the WDPA on Upari, a free open-source globe: is this use non-commercial?

---

Dear Protected Planet team,

I make Upari (https://upari.earth), a 3D globe that tells the Earth as it is today: forests,
mangroves, the intactness of nature, the sea. It is free, with no ads, no accounts, no
tracking and no paid tier, and its code is open source under the Apache License 2.0
(https://github.com/enuzzo/upari). It shows only open data, and every source is credited on
screen, with a link to its holder, wherever its data appear.

I would like to add the world's protected areas to the nature chapter, next to the forests
lost since 2001 and the Biodiversity Intactness Index: where nature is protected, and where
it is not. I have read your terms and want to be sure this use fits them, for two reasons.

1. The site carries the name of Netmilk Studio, my small design studio, as a signature. The
   studio sells nothing through Upari and earns nothing from it. Do you consider this use
   non-commercial?
2. Upari would show the WDPA only as a picture: the protected areas rasterised once into a
   coloured layer on the globe (about 5 km per pixel), committed to the repository as an
   image. No polygons, attributes or downloads would be offered, which I understand is
   allowed ("the WDPCA Data are not downloadable"). Does a pre-rendered image built by our
   public build count as publishing, or as redistribution?

The credit would read, on the page that shows the layer:

> UNEP-WCMC and IUCN (2026), Protected Planet: The World Database on Protected Areas (WDPA),
> [month] 2026, Cambridge, UK: UNEP-WCMC and IUCN. www.protectedplanet.net

I am glad to send you the page for review before it is published, and to provide the two
copies your terms ask for (here, two links). If the answer is yes, a one-line reply is
enough, and I will record it, with the date, in the project's third-party notices.
